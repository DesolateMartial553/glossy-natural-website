Experience the natural goodness of Coconut Oil, a versatile, nourishing oil designed to help keep your skin and hair soft, smooth, and well-conditioned.

Rich in skin-loving oils, it helps moisturise dry skin, leaving it feeling soft and supple, while also providing nourishment and conditioning for dry or brittle hair. Its lightweight versatility makes it an excellent addition to your everyday beauty and self-care routine.

Why you'll love it

  • Helps moisturize and soften dry skin
  • Helps keep skin feeling smooth and supple
  • Nourishes and conditions dry hair
  • Helps improve the feel of dry, rough areas
  • Versatile for everyday skin and hair care
  • Suitable for use as part of your regular beauty routine

How to use

For the skin, apply a small amount to clean, slightly damp skin and massage gently until absorbed. For hair, apply a small amount to the scalp or hair strands as desired, focusing on dry areas.

Caution

  • For external use only.
  • Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
  • Patch test recommended before first use.
  • Keep away from children. Store in a cool, dry place.
