Our Exfoliating Body Scrub is thoughtfully formulated to help remove dead skin cells and surface buildup while leaving your skin feeling refreshed and renewed.

Its gentle exfoliating action helps improve the feel and appearance of rough, dull skin, while nourishing ingredients help leave your skin feeling soft, smooth, and beautifully conditioned.

Perfect for your regular body-care routine, it helps create a smoother canvas for moisturisers and other skincare products while giving your skin that fresh, polished feel.

Why you'll love it

  • Gently exfoliates and removes dead skin cells
  • Helps improve the appearance of dull, rough-looking skin
  • Leaves skin feeling soft, smooth, and refreshed
  • Helps enhance the skin's natural-looking glow
  • Prepares the skin for better application of body-care products
  • Suitable for regular body-care routines

How to use

On damp skin, scoop a generous amount and massage gently in circular motions all over your body. Rinse thoroughly. Use 3x a week for optimal results.

Caution

  • For external use only.
  • Do not use on broken or irritated skin.
  • Avoid harsh scrubbing — gentle hands, glowing results.
  • Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
  • Keep away from children. Store in a cool, dry place.

Use consistently as part of your complete body-care routine, 2-3 times weekly.
