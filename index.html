Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?
Message us directly on WhatsApp with the product(s) you'd like, and we'll confirm price and arrange delivery or pickup from our GRA location in Enugu.
Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?
Our formulas are made to be gentle for everyday use, but we always recommend a small patch test first, and to discontinue use if any irritation occurs — especially with exfoliating or active products.
Do you deliver outside Enugu?
Yes — send us your location on WhatsApp and we'll let you know the delivery options and cost for your area.
How should I store my products?
Keep all products in a cool, dry place, out of reach of children, and away from direct sunlight to preserve freshness.
