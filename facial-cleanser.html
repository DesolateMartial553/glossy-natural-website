Give blemish-prone skin a fresh, balanced start with our Anti-Acne Facial Cleanser, specially formulated to gently cleanse away excess oil, dirt, impurities, and buildup without leaving the skin feeling excessively dry.

With consistent use, it helps keep the skin feeling clean and refreshed while supporting a clearer, smoother, and healthier-looking complexion. It is an excellent addition to a daily skincare routine for individuals dealing with oily or acne-prone skin.

Why you'll love it

  • Gently cleanses dirt, oil, and impurities
  • Helps control excess oil
  • Helps keep pores clean
  • Supports a clearer-looking complexion
  • Helps reduce the appearance of blemish-prone skin
  • Leaves the skin feeling fresh, clean, and refreshed
  • Suitable for oily and acne-prone skin

How to use

Wet your face with clean water. Apply a small amount of cleanser and gently massage over the face in circular motions, avoiding the eye area. Rinse thoroughly and pat dry. Follow with your preferred moisturiser and other skincare products.

Caution

  • For external use only.
  • Discontinue use if irritation occurs.
  • Patch test recommended before first use.
  • Keep away from children. Store in a cool, dry place.
